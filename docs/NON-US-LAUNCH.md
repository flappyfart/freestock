# Intended non-US DeFi launch

The active product is DeFi earnings directed to Stock Token purchases or reinvestment. The old prize/draw design is superseded. freestock currently accepts practice funds only. No country has been activated for real financial transactions.

`lib/launch-policy.ts` retains an informational issuer-restriction self-check for residence, current location and Regulation S US-person status. Answers are not sent or stored. Passing this self-check does not authorize a deposit or purchase. Stock Tokens are economic exposures, not underlying share ownership.

Live activation requires supported jurisdictions and a verified transaction path per strategy: position accounting, withdrawals, available net earnings, costs and loss recovery, debt buffers where relevant, quotes, token status, signed permissions and reconciliation. Auto-conversion and compounding must be bounded by the user's preferences and executable liquidity. Staking rewards may not be immediately claimable; leveraged LP support needs an actual borrowing and collateral route.

The app's Learn, Docs and Transparency pages contain the current user-facing mechanics and limitations. Current integration: read-only Morpho data; lending and LP practice positions; single-token and basket allocations; simulated threshold conversions and compounding. No fund movement or background automation.
