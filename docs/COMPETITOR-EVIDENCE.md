# Freestock positioning: competitor evidence

Prepared 9 September 2026 (UTC). Internal working document; observations are dated and may change. This is a comparison of supported product claims and bounded deployment checks, not an endorsement or a claim that Freestock is first or unique.

## Proposed customer and promise

**First customer:** a person already lending USDG or another supported stablecoin, who intends to retain a lending position and wants to accumulate specific Stock Tokens with the earnings. They understand wallet approvals and currently decide manually whether to reinvest or move earnings elsewhere.

**Promise to test:** “Keep lending. Turn the interest into a stock portfolio.”

This describes the intended experience. Current Freestock creates its own supported lending account; connecting an arbitrary existing position is a future capability. Stock Tokens are the purchased asset, rather than shares held in a brokerage account.

## What is and is not differentiated

**Already claimed elsewhere:** stock baskets, compounding, selectable payout assets, automated routing, yield optimization, non-custodial interfaces and transparent contract links. Equinox also explicitly describes converting LP/lending earnings into a chosen stock, with persistent elections, small-payment deferral, slippage carryover and epoch accounting. Its undeployed status does not make those concepts original to Freestock.

**Specific customer workflow to validate:** retain a stablecoin lending position, direct only a defined available-earnings budget into a chosen Stock Token allocation, and skip conversions whose costs exceed the user's limit. This is a focused experience to build and prove, not an assertion that the feature combination is unique. The comparison is bounded; it cannot establish that no other live product does it.

**Potential defensibility:** reliable lending adapters; a comprehensible earnings ledger; execution limits that users can revoke; accurate cost previews; and recovery that reconciles pending transactions. These are engineering and operating capabilities to demonstrate, not present-day claims of a moat.

Avoid claiming that keeping a lending position guarantees principal value. Define the spending budget precisely. Current surplus accounting does not prove that every available unit came from borrower interest. Economic losses, liquidity constraints and execution costs still matter.
